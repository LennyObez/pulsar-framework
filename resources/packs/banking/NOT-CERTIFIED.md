# Important Disclaimer

## This Pack Is NOT a Compliance Certification

This control pack provides a **starting point** for building financial applications. It is **NOT** certified compliant with any regulation, standard, or framework, including but not limited to:

- **PCI-DSS** (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
- **PSD2** (Payment Services Directive 2)
- **DORA** (Digital Operational Resilience Act)
- **SOX** (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)
- **BSA/AML** (Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering)

## What This Pack Provides

- Entity scaffolds for common financial domain objects (transactions, accounts, payment intents, KYC profiles)
- Configuration stubs for encryption, audit logging, and PCI-compliant logging
- A starting directory structure for financial applications
- Test stubs to validate your domain logic

## What This Pack Does NOT Provide

- A certified or audited compliance implementation
- Guaranteed protection against data breaches or fraud
- A substitute for professional security assessment
- Legal advice or regulatory guidance
- Production-ready encryption, tokenization, or key management
- Certified payment processing capabilities

## Your Responsibilities

Before processing any financial data in production, you **must**:

1. Engage a Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) for PCI-DSS compliance
2. Implement proper encryption key management with an HSM or certified KMS
3. Complete all applicable Self-Assessment Questionnaires (SAQs)
4. Establish incident response and breach notification procedures
5. Obtain all required licenses for financial services in your jurisdiction
6. Consult legal counsel regarding regulatory requirements
