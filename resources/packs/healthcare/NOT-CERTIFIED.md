# Important Disclaimer

## This Pack Is NOT a Compliance Certification

This control pack provides a **starting point** for building healthcare applications. It is **NOT** certified compliant with any regulation, standard, or framework, including but not limited to:

- **HIPAA** (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
- **HITECH** (Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act)
- **MDR** (Medical Device Regulation — EU 2017/745)
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 11** (Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures)
- **GDPR** (as it applies to health data)

## What This Pack Provides

- Entity scaffolds for common healthcare domain objects (patients, medical records, appointments, prescriptions)
- Configuration stubs for PHI access logging, data classification, and encryption
- Integration point stubs for HL7/FHIR interoperability
- Test stubs to validate your domain logic

## What This Pack Does NOT Provide

- A HIPAA-compliant implementation
- Protected Health Information (PHI) handling that meets regulatory requirements
- A substitute for a HIPAA Security Risk Assessment
- Validated software suitable for clinical use
- Medical device certification or CE marking
- Legal advice or regulatory guidance

## Patient Safety Warning

If this software will be used in clinical settings or will process, store, or transmit Protected Health Information (PHI), you **must** ensure full regulatory compliance before any clinical deployment. Failure to do so may endanger patient safety and violate federal and state law.

## Your Responsibilities

Before handling any PHI or deploying in a healthcare setting, you **must**:

1. Complete a HIPAA Security Risk Assessment
2. Establish Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all applicable vendors
3. Designate HIPAA Privacy and Security Officers
4. Implement workforce training on HIPAA requirements
5. Establish incident response and breach notification procedures
6. If applicable, engage a Notified Body for MDR CE marking
7. Consult legal counsel regarding all applicable regulations
