# Important Disclaimer

## This Pack Is NOT a Compliance Certification

This scaffolding pack provides a **starting point** for building legal practice management applications. It is **NOT** certified compliant with any regulation, standard, bar association rule, or legal industry requirement.

## What This Pack Provides

- Entity scaffolds for common legal domain objects (cases, documents, clients, deadlines)
- Configuration stubs for document retention policies and audit logging
- Attorney-client privilege flag support on documents
- Test stubs to validate your domain logic

## What This Pack Does NOT Provide

- A system that guarantees compliance with bar association rules
- Attorney-client privilege protection (technical controls alone are insufficient)
- Conflict checking capabilities
- Trust accounting or escrow management
- E-filing integration with courts
- Legal advice or regulatory guidance

## Your Responsibilities

Before using this software in a legal practice setting, you **must**:

1. Review applicable bar association rules for electronic records and communication
2. Establish document retention and destruction policies approved by counsel
3. Implement conflict checking procedures
4. Ensure proper trust accounting controls (where applicable)
5. Train all staff on confidentiality and privilege requirements
6. Consult with a legal technology specialist regarding compliance
