# Important Disclaimer

## This Pack Is NOT a Compliance Certification

This scaffolding pack provides a **starting point** for building multi-tenant SaaS applications. It is **NOT** certified compliant with any regulation, standard, or security framework, including but not limited to:

- **SOC 2** (Service Organization Control 2)
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **ISO 27001** (Information Security Management)

## What This Pack Provides

- Entity scaffolds for common SaaS domain objects (tenants, subscriptions, plans, features)
- Configuration stubs for multi-tenancy, billing, and feature flags
- A starting directory structure for SaaS applications
- Test stubs to validate your domain logic

## What This Pack Does NOT Provide

- Guaranteed tenant data isolation
- A production-ready billing system
- Data residency compliance
- A substitute for SOC 2 audit preparation
- Legal advice or regulatory guidance

## Your Responsibilities

Before launching a SaaS product, you **must**:

1. Conduct a thorough security audit of tenant isolation boundaries
2. Integrate with a certified payment provider for billing
3. Review data residency requirements for all target markets
4. Implement proper data export and deletion procedures
5. Establish incident response and data breach notification procedures
6. Consult legal counsel regarding applicable privacy regulations

The authors and contributors of this pack accept **no liability** for data breaches, tenant data leakage, billing errors, or legal consequences arising from the use of this software. Use at your own risk and with appropriate professional guidance.
